### Summary

This study introduces the PAthoentity Shuffle Strategy (PASS) for pathology-image analysis. By shuffling biologically meaningful image regions, it encourages neural networks to capture morphological relationships within and across samples. Experiments on 10 datasets covering different diseases, organs, and magnification levels demonstrate its effectiveness.
