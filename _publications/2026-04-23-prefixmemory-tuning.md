### Summary

Conventional prefix-tuning can suffer from a tradeoff between the influence of input tokens and the learned prefix within attention. PrefixMemory-Tuning addresses this limitation by moving the prefix module outside the attention mechanism, improving its expressiveness and showing competitive performance against contemporary parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods.
