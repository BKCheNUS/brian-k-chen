**Authors:** **Brian K. Chen**, Tianyang Hu, Hui Jin, Hwee Kuan Lee, and Kenji Kawaguchi.

**Venue:** NeurIPS 2024 Workshop on Adaptive Foundation Models: Evolving AI for Personalized and Efficient Learning (poster).

### Summary

We study the relationship between in-context learning and gradient-based optimization in linearized self-attention. For a single layer, we establish an equivalence to a gradient-descent step on a prompt-dependent dataset. In deeper models, the mechanism resembles greedy layer-wise gradient descent, and we extend the analysis to causal, autoregressive attention.
