**Authors:** **Brian K. Chen**, Tianyang Hu, Hui Jin, Hwee Kuan Lee, and Kenji Kawaguchi.

**Venue:** 41st International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML 2024), Proceedings of Machine Learning Research, volume 235, pages 6833–6846.

### Summary

This paper shows how the effect of in-context demonstration examples can be incorporated exactly into a linearized-attention transformer by adding bias terms, without costly gradient-based updates. We also explore an approximate adaptation of the technique to standard transformers and evaluate it on GPT-2.
